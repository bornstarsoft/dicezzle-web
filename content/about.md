Dicezzle is a lightweight web puzzle game from Bornstar Soft. It is designed for quick mobile sessions: place dice, merge matching values, create Star Dice, and keep the board open as long as you can.

The MVP is static-first, browser-based, and respectful by design. There is no login, no purchases, no gacha, no casino framing, and no forced ads.
