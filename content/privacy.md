Dicezzle is built to be privacy-conscious.

## Local game data

The MVP stores simple game preferences and scores in your browser using localStorage:

- `dicezzle.bestScore`
- `dicezzle.soundEnabled`
- `dicezzle.tutorialDismissed`
- `dicezzle.totalGames`
- `dicezzle.lastResult`

This data stays on your device unless you clear browser storage.

## Analytics

The MVP includes a safe analytics wrapper but does not include any analytics ID, tracking pixel, login system, or personal information collection by default.

## Sharing

The Share and Copy buttons create text on your device. Dicezzle does not require an account to share a result.

## Contact

This policy may be updated before public launch. Contact details should be updated with the official Bornstar Soft channel before release.
