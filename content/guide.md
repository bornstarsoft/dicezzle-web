## 1. Place dice on the board

Dicezzle uses a 5x5 board. Tap a die in the tray, then tap an empty cell to place it.

## 2. Merge three matching dice

Three or more matching dice merge when they touch up, down, left, or right. Diagonal dice do not connect.

## 3. Create Star Dice

Merging three or more sixes creates a Star Die.

## 4. Use Star Clears

Three or more connected Star Dice trigger a Star Clear. The star group disappears and clears a 3x3 area around the merge target.

## 5. Keep space open

Open space matters. Think about where the next three dice can go, and try to set up chains that reopen the board.

## 6. Share your result

At game over, Dicezzle shows your score, best die, Star Clears, best chain, and rank. Use Share or Copy result to challenge friends naturally.
