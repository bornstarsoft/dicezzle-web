What is Dicezzle?

Dicezzle is a free dice merge puzzle online. Place dice on a 5x5 board, merge matching connected dice, and keep your board open.

How to play

Tap a tray die, tap an empty cell, and match three connected dice. Sixes become Star Dice, and Star Dice can clear space.

Why it is fun

Each turn is quick, but every placement matters. Good chains feel satisfying because they create space right when the board gets tight.

Play on mobile or desktop

The game is built for touch screens first, with large cells, readable dice, and no stressful timer.

Share your score

Game over creates a clean result card with score, best die, Star Clears, best chain, and rank.

Classic first, Daily later

Classic mode is playable now. Daily Dice is planned after the core puzzle is stable.
